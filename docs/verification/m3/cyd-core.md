# CYD portable controller

The native and ESP-IDF targets share the same production `vault_device` and `vault_display` sources. The native tests run with a monotonic fake clock, complete protocol messages, a fake transport, logical touch samples and an instrumented canvas. They do not enable board pins, open the physical display or confirm ADC calibration.

- `device_test.cpp` covers welcome/public snapshot ordering, explicit role claim, board version and role/epoch fencing, same-envelope retries at 500 ms (three retries), send failure and silent peer teardown. It checks that hold input is independent of movement and rune commands, that delayed challenges do not revive a hold, and that role change, pause, touch loss and reconnect all require a stable lift followed by fresh contact. The game position and rune selection only change with the authorized server projection.
- `touch_test.cpp` exercises each direction, disallowed/diagonal/reversing swipes, no repeat while sliding, rune/hold separation and contact invalidation. A maze gesture begins on the maze area and sends at most one move after a stable release. The initial software thresholds are 24 logical pixels, at least 1.5:1 directional dominance, 30 ms stable down and 50 ms stable lift. HOLD SEAL starts on stable down and ends immediately on release, driver loss, leaving its target plus a 5 px margin, or 100 ms without a sample. Physical tuning is pending.
- `display_test.cpp` checks portrait primitive bounds, unknown versus known-open walls, local and accepted feedback, an active hold at zero elapsed, disabled/offline overlays, simulated release labels, touch target sizes and the display self-test. Its calibration checks use synthetic 12-bit ADC data: four corners, a center check, axis inversion/swap, jitter or invalid center rejection, touch loss and profile mismatch. The wizard waits for at least 100 ms stable contact and 50 ms release at each target, verifies five fitted coordinates within 12 px, and returns versioned coefficients bound to the active profile hash. No physical touch accuracy follows from these tests.
- The portable diagnostics screen shows current and minimum free internal heap, the largest available internal block, and the minimum unused stack for the main task and network worker. Values use bytes. The stack rows also show the configured size and free percentage. A worker that has yet to start shows **Unavailable**. Touch and setup rows use fixed status labels. The renderer takes numeric samples and status enums through the display interface. The sanitizer display test checked its portrait bounds, real zero headroom, unavailable stack samples, and the widest supported numbers.
- Network diagnostics show a fixed phase and stage label and available numeric SDK, Wi-Fi, HTTP, socket, and TLS codes. The calibration footer shows the current phase and stage plus one available code. Its bounds leave the touch targets and instructions visible. The native sanitizer test checked the code display and footer bounds. Physical readings require the board adapter.
- Calibration feedback shows whether a raw touch point reached the wizard, the coordinates and pressure or driver error, and the longest gap between polls reported by the adapter. The wizard reports when to press or lift and shows the last rejection while waiting for a new attempt. The sanitizer display test checked the rejection and capture paths and verified that the three rows leave the calibration targets and color swatches clear.
- The wizard lets a finger settle after landing. An ADC span above 32 counts restarts the steady interval. Capture uses the most recent 100 ms of observed steady down samples and a 50 ms lift. A late movement restarts the interval, and a lost, delayed, or backward-clock sample requires a new lift and contact. The native display test checked these cases and repeated the five-point fit.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
cmake -S firmware -B firmware/build-host -DVAULT_SANITIZE=ON
cmake --build firmware/build-host --parallel 6
ctest --test-dir firmware/build-host --output-on-failure
```

Executed with CMake 4.1.3 and AppleClang 21.0.0 on the host with address and undefined behavior sanitizers. The integrated native run passed all seven suites: JSON, protocol, device, touch, display, transport and controller service. Physical LCD color and orientation, ADC behavior, lift/repress under actual driver loss and Wi-Fi interruption, and electrical/power-bank checks remain pending on two verified profiles.
